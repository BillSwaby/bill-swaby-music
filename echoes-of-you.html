ECHOES OF YOU

Bill Swaby
If My Heart Could Speak

A song about the memories that remain after love has gone — hearing someone's voice in familiar places, remembering the words left unsaid, and trying to find a way forward while still living among the echoes of yesterday.

LYRICS

I walk the streets where we used to go,
Your shadow lingers, though I’m all alone.
Every corner whispers your name,
But nothing feels the same.

I reach for your hand in the dark,
But all I find is an empty spark.

And I’m still waiting,
For a love that’s gone away.
I’m still chasing,
The ghost of yesterday.
All the words we didn’t say,
Haunt me every day,
And I’m lost in the echoes of you.

I hear your laugh in the pouring rain,

Your pictures fade, but the ache remains,
A melody I can’t untame.

I close my eyes to see your face,
But time won’t let me stay in that place.

Maybe one day, the hurt will fade,
And I’ll find the sun in a different shade.

A heart half-empty, half-stuck on you.

And I’m still waiting,

But it’s only the echoes of you.
