IF MY HEART COULD SPEAK

Album by Bill Swaby

If My Heart Could Speak by Bill Swaby is an 11-song collection of original music about love, memories, missed chances, heartbreak and the words we sometimes struggle to say. Each song explores a different emotion, from the laughter of memories to the echoes of a love that refuses to disappear.
