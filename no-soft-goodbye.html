NO SOFT GOODBYE

Bill Swaby
The Rhythm Of My Heart

A raw and uncompromising song about being left behind, confronting the truth and refusing to pretend that heartbreak was something easy to walk away from.

LYRICS

You said we’d be different,
You swore you were sure,
Funny how certainty
Always has an escape door.
You held me like forever
Wasn’t a dangerous word,
Then left like commitment
Was something you’d never heard.

You called it bad timing,
I call it convenient lies.

I loved you like it mattered,
You loved me like a phase.
Like something you could put down
When it started to weigh.
Now I’m choking on the quiet
You said I’d understand,
If that was your version of love,
You can have it back — keep the plan.

You said I was “too much,”
Said I felt things too deep,
But you fed off my fire
Then complained about the heat.
You took all my patience,
Left me learning the cost,
Guess it’s easy to move on
When you’re not the one lost.

You get to be fine,
I get to be honest.

I loved you like it mattered,
You loved me when it was easy.
You called it self-preservation,
I call it leaving me bleeding.
Now every song you once was
Sounds empty, rehearsed,
Hope you find what you’re chasing
Just know I felt it first.

Maybe I was naive,
Maybe I believed too hard,
But loving you wasn’t the mistake
Staying quiet was the hardest part.
I hope your next “almost forever”
Asks you to stay when it’s dark,
’Cause running only works
When you don’t have a heart.

I loved you like it mattered,
Like I didn’t have a choice.
You loved me with one foot out,
Already gone in your voice.
So here’s the truth you dodged back then,
No soft goodbye, no disguise:
I wasn’t hard to love
You were just scared to try.
