A tender and emotional song about two people accepting that their love has changed — and finding the courage to let each other go with sadness, dignity, and gentleness.

LYRICS

They stood beneath a fading sky,
Two shadows where one used to be.
He spoke her name like something soft,
A memory slipping free.

She held his hands a moment more,
As if the warmth could change their fate.
But love had shifted, grown apart—
Too fragile now to carry weight.

Their laughter lived in quiet rooms,
In echoes only they could hear.
But time had worn the colors thin,
And truth had finally pulled them clear.

No anger rose, no bitter flame—
Just tears that neither tried to hide.
A silent nod, a trembling breath,
Two tender hearts that once aligned.

They let each other go that day,
With one last touch that said enough.
For even broken love can end
With gentleness instead of rough.

And as they walked their separate ways,
The moon climbed up, a solemn guide
Two stories drifting into night,
Once intertwined,
Now untied.
