THOUGH YOU'RE GONE

Bill Swaby
Still Loving You — Song 06

An emotional song about missing someone who is no longer there — holding onto their memories, their voice, and the love that remains even when they are gone.

LYRICS

The night is quiet, but I still hear your name,

I reach for the place where your hand used to be,
And the silence falls heavy, surrounding me.

Though you’re gone, my heart still calls,
Through the shadows, through the walls.
Love doesn’t end when the night grows long,
You live in my soul, you live in my song.

The photographs tremble in the morning light,
I keep them close to get me through the night.
The world keeps turning, but I stand still,
Holding to a love I always will.

Though you’re gone, my heart still calls,
Through the shadows, through the walls.
Love doesn’t end when the night grows long,
You live in my soul, you live in my song.

If I could cross the stars, I’d find you there,
Wrapped in the warmth of a timeless prayer.
No goodbye can break what we’ve begun,
Our love shines brighter than the sun.

Though you’re gone, my heart still calls,
Through the silence, through it all.
Love never fades, it just carries on,
You live in my soul, you live in my song.
