YOU'RE GONE BUT MY LOVE STAYS

Bill Swaby
Still Loving You
An emotional song about losing someone you love, holding onto their memory, and discovering that true love can remain even when the person is no longer there.

LYRICS

I still hear your voice at night,
Soft as the glow of a fading light.
I reach for you, but you’re not there,
Only shadows and the empty air.

You’re gone, but my love stays,
Living in me in quiet ways.
Though the world moves on, I can’t let go,
You’re in my heart  that’s all I know.

Your picture rests beside my bed,
I trace your smile, the words you said.
Time may steal, but it can’t erase,
The gentle warmth of your embrace.

You’re gone, but my love stays,
Living in me in quiet ways.
Though the world moves on, I can’t let go,
You’re in my heart  that’s all I know.

If I could find a way across the sky,
I’d hold you close, I’d never say goodbye.
But love is stronger than the hands of time,
And still, I call you mine.

You’re gone, but my love stays,
Guiding me through my darkest days.
Though I can’t touch you, I still know,
You’re in my heart   that’s all I know.
